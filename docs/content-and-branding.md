## Included examples

The Studio demo demonstrates a generic workshop workflow. Its diagram is self-contained and its sample commands only print a greeting. The layout sampler uses synthetic material. No vendor product decks, documentation-derived Q&A, detection results, logos, or branded presets are bundled.

## User-supplied presentations

Users can supply their own text, logos, images, and videos. They are responsible for permissions and accurate statements of ownership and affiliation. Do not imply vendor endorsement or act as an authorized representative without authorization. Imports are user-provided content; the app does not certify their claims.

Older project files remain importable through an identifier-only adapter. Their intentional content and branding stay with those files. New exports use neutral project identifiers. Imported projects retain their own reset baseline; they do not become bundled templates.
