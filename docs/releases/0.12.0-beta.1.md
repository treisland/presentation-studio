## Authoring and playback

- Use purpose-built SVG Orbit/Aurora/Waves compositions, inner ribbon flow and coordinated decorative palettes, preserving custom hues, monochrome themes and the three-color Constellation correction. Compare an inert Current slide preview with a neutral sample before applying changes.
- Choose a style variation and Subtle/Balanced/Expressive strength, or retain exact custom values through advanced controls. These shortcuts map to existing settings without new saved fields or another format change. Motion status clearly distinguishes Static, playback animation, active preview and accessibility pauses.

- Choose Orbit, Aurora, Waves, Constellation or Geometric Drift through Project settings → Background, with staged visual previews and one-step Apply/Undo. Configure a presentation default or a slide-specific override through Inspector → Slide settings → Content → Background.
- Distinguish None, Static and Animated. Keep independent grid visibility, quiet content areas, placement, bounded intensity/cycle/scale and standard/simpler rendering. Semantic theme colors follow light/dark/custom palettes; explicit hex slots remain custom until Reset to theme.
- Refine preset visibility after manual review: stronger layered Waves strokes and ribbon shading, a connected Constellation network with brighter points, clearer filled/ring effects, and lightened theme-linked decoration on dark slides. Custom colors and quiet content areas remain under user control.
- Match the concept more closely with flowing vector wave ribbons, layered chromatic Aurora veils and a denser, gently drifting constellation. Move Background immediately after Presentation settings. Fix slide-autoplay pause freezing background motion; the separate background switch and accessibility/media-focus safeguards still govern animation. Static mode explains why Preview motion is disabled.
- Editor canvases and navigator thumbnails stay static. Explicit motion previews and viewer motion controls are session-only, respect reduced-motion/Disable motion, and pause inactive instances. Matching resolved backgrounds retain stage continuity; changed effects restart at a deterministic phase.
- Preserve offline editable ZIP, playback and independent booth use. Include validated background IDs/options in the generated AI schema/catalog and a new background-effects.json example. Imported content remains data, with no custom executable uploads or remote effect services.

## Guarded compatibility and recovery

The maintainer explicitly approved native schema v3 for effect-enabled projects and their recoverable history/checkpoints/starting state. Untouched projects continue exporting v2, and v0.12.0 retains existing v1/v2 imports. Older editors reject v3 instead of silently discarding its settings. Do not downgrade version numbers; preserve the original and use a compatible Studio build. See [import compatibility](../import-compatibility.md).

Editable ZIPs preserve identities, media, supported settings and recovery/history. Playback/booth exports omit hidden/omitted slide overrides and private authoring content. Browser recovery is separate from portable backups. Save an editable ZIP before moving between beta/stable origins or browser profiles.

## Validation and limits

The implementation has explicit bounds: one animated stage effect, at most 24 decorative nodes per layer, zero animated navigator thumbnails and no effect-specific per-frame JavaScript loop. Simpler rendering caps particles at six. These structural bounds and automated 50-slide continuity coverage do not certify every device, video codec, OBS version or extreme custom palette.

Review readability throughout motion, especially with dense text, tables/code and real video. Custom intensity/palettes can reduce contrast. Personal saved effect presets, interactive backgrounds, new canvas formats and arbitrary JS/CSS/shader uploads remain outside scope. No dependency or external service is added.
