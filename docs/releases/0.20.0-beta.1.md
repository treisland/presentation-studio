# Presentation Studio 0.20.0-beta.1

This maintainer-accepted beta combines the 0.20 media-authoring preview with milestone 34 canvas reliability improvements.

- Select overlapping objects through named Select underneath choices with preview outlines; blank object padding and decorative descendants resolve to their owner.
- Keep Layers batch selection and Inspector context synchronized. Preserve direct text/table editing and specialized Chart studio, crop and group-member workflows.
- Reduce repeated release-time view/layout work, honor pointer ownership and final endpoint coordinates, and keep arrow endpoint grips reachable on their geometry.
- Choose Slide or Selection bounds for alignment. Groups count once for spacing and retain their internal geometry. Snap guides explain their alignment.
- Retain the alpha's narration recording, advancement defaults/overrides, original-media inventory, guarded deletion/replacement and measured audio previews.

## Limits and compatibility

The 50 ms release target remains unmet. Instrumented simple fixtures measured roughly 80–120 ms and dense fixtures 240–724 ms; these are observations under local validation load, not an SLA. Personal compositions and large decks may need additional tuning. Do not infer new browser/codec or assistive-technology certification from automated checks.

Existing IDs, v1–v6 native imports, original media, recovery, atomic Undo and editable/playback/booth offline reopening remain supported under the existing contracts. No dependency or new format is introduced by the canvas work. Keep complete editable ZIP backups; exporting playback is not a project backup. The separate stable and beta origins do not share browser storage.

## Delivery

Beta publication and beta Pages deployment require protected integration/promotion, exact-main Windows/Linux checks, a successful immutable tag workflow and four verified assets. Stable 0.20.0 and the companion release listing follow only after those gates and the maintainer's conditional production authorization. See [validation and authority](0.20.0-beta.1-validation.md).
